# Activity 4 Worksheet — Circularity and Decarbonisation Portfolio

## Exact procedure

1. Open `mock-data/mock-data.csv`. Separate negative annual cost (a saving) from positive annual cost.
2. Calculate marginal abatement cost: `MAC = annual_net_cost_sgd / annual_abatement_tco2e`. Worked example for compressed-air optimisation: `-85,000 / 420 = -S$202.38/tCO2e`.
3. Rank initiatives from lowest to highest MAC, but mark dependencies and delivery time before selecting the sequence.
4. Quantify portfolio abatement by year; do not add an initiative until its dependency and start date are satisfied.
5. Compare MAC with Singapore’s carbon-tax context: S$45/tCO2e for 2026–2027. Carbon tax alone is not a complete investment case.
6. Classify the sequence: no-regret (negative MAC), enabling (creates later capability), transformation (material business-model change).
7. State ecological and community effects, evidence owner and verification method for each selected initiative.
